Avalon, New Jersey · Since 1913

Neighbors protecting neighbors

The Avalon Volunteer Fire Department answers fire, rescue and water emergencies across Avalon and stands ready to help our mutual-aid partners up and down Cape May County.

Fire inspections

Inspections are conducted by certified inspectors of the Borough of Avalon Construction Office. The fire department cannot perform this service. Contact the Fire Inspection Office.
