February 12, 2015

5th Avenue structure fire

Shortly before 11:00 p.m., Avalon Fire was dispatched to the 4800 block of 5th Avenue for a reported structure fire. High winds and frigid temperatures hampered firefighting; a new home under construction and an unoccupied vacation home nearby were destroyed. AVFD thanks the fifteen neighboring fire companies who helped with water supply and manpower that night.
