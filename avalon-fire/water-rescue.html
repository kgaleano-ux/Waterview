Water Rescue

Avalon is surrounded by water on three sides. Information about the department’s water rescue work is on its way.

Page coming soon

We’re putting together details on our water rescue team, its equipment and training. Check back soon.

In the meantime, questions can go to the firehouse at 609-967-4411.

Someone in trouble in the water? Call 911 right away. Point to the person and keep your eyes on them until help arrives. Throw something that floats, and don’t go in after them unless you are trained to.
